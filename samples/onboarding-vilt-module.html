Learning Design Work Sample | ADDIE + vILT Facilitation + Microlearning

Remote Onboarding Curriculum: Accurate Record Handoffs & AI Draft Review

A public instructional design sample modeled on how I train distributed frontline specialists: combining clear business outcomes, structured 45-minute live virtual facilitation (vILT), Loom and Scribe visual job aids, and measurable post-session validation.

Design Context (Non-Proprietary Sample): Built specifically for remote support, member-services, and operations teams using CRM/ticketing tools alongside generative AI assistants (Claude / Gemini). Zero confidential employer data is included.

1. ADDIE Instructional Design Architecture

Click each ADDIE phase to inspect design decisions, SME collaboration checkpoints, and selected modalities.

Audience, Gap Analysis & Performance Goal

  • Target Learners: Distributed remote frontline specialists and points of contact working live escalations across CRM, ticketing, and external partner workflows.
  • Observed Gap: Escalation handoffs stall when specialists pass unverified symptoms or unedited AI summaries downstream without checking source records first.
  • Learning Objective: Given an ambiguous escalation or AI-drafted note, learners apply a 4-point source-of-truth check, select the correct decision-tree route, and complete a confirmed warm handoff.

Blended Modality Selection (Why Each Format Fits)

  • Pre-Work (10 min async): Self-paced 1-page Scribe step-by-step job aid + short Loom video modeling one real record check so live class time focuses on practice instead of lecture.
  • vILT Workshop (45 min live): Facilitated group practice on edge cases, live screen share navigation, and structured peer critique of AI-drafted summaries.
  • On-the-Job Reinforcement: Clickable decision tree pinned in the Notion intranet beside existing ticket templates.

Content Development & AI Output Validation

  • AI Drafting Workflow: Use Claude or Gemini to draft alternative scenario variants and knowledge-check distractors quickly from SME bullet points.
  • Human Validation Gate: Every AI-generated draft undergoes a mandatory accuracy, tone, inclusivity, and policy check against primary SOPs before SME sign-off (see AI Eval Rubric).
  • Accessibility & Clarity: Short action-first headings, plain language, captioned Loom clips, and high-contrast visual job aids for rapid scanning during live interactions.

Facilitation & Distributed Delivery Plan

  • Cohort Size: 8 to 15 remote learners via video session with active chat prompts every 5 to 7 minutes to keep engagement high.
  • Trainer Toolkit: Facilitator run-of-show, timer cues, cold-call-free volunteer prompts, and backup async Loom recording + quiz for scheduling conflicts.
  • SME Co-Sign: Operations team lead joins last 10 minutes of pilot cohort to capture edge cases needing SOP updates.

Kirkpatrick-Aligned Evaluation Plan

  • Level 1 (Reaction): 2-question end-of-session pulse check on clarity and confidence applying the decision tree live.
  • Level 2 (Learning): Scenario knowledge check integrated at the bottom of this module (requiring accurate identification of escalation vs. self-correction steps).
  • Level 3 & 4 (Transfer & Quality): QA review of live ticket handoff notes across weeks 1 and 2 post-training to confirm missing-field and back-and-forth clarifications trend downward.

2. Live Virtual Training (vILT) Facilitator Run-of-Show (45 Minutes)

Time Segment Modality / Tool Facilitator Cue & Learner Action
00:00 - 05:00 Opening Hook & Outcomes vILT + Chat Waterfall Ask: "What makes an escalated ticket bounce back to you?" Synthesize replies into the single source-of-truth checklist.
05:00 - 15:00 Model the Standard Live Screen Share + Scribe Job Aid Walk through one conflicting-record case live in sandbox/reference view. Explicitly point out where to stop and verify dates/owners.
15:00 - 28:00 Guided Case Practice + AI Check Breakout / Poll + AI Diff View Present an AI-drafted summary containing a plausible but unverified claim. Learners spot the defect and rewrite using primary facts.
28:00 - 40:00 Warm-Handoff Roleplay Live Audio / Chat Handoff Two volunteers run a 2-minute live warm handoff: confirm receiving owner by name, state verified facts, and record closure owner.
40:00 - 45:00 Action Plan & Knowledge Check Intranet Link + Quiz Pin Notion job aid + decision tree; learners complete the 4-question check below and submit pulse feedback before closing.

3. Interactive Learner Knowledge Check

Question 1: You use an AI assistant to summarize a 12-message ticket before escalating to a partner lead. The draft inserts a fast SLA figure that does not appear anywhere in the thread. What do you do first?

Question 2: When handling an urgent warm handoff to an external or on-call contact, when is your responsibility complete?
