Learning Design Sample | ADDIE Blueprint, vILT Guide & Job Aid

Remote Onboarding Curriculum: Accurate Record Handoffs & AI Draft Review

A public instructional design sample modeling how I structure training for distributed teams using ADDIE (Analyze, Design, Develop, Implement, Evaluate), live virtual instructor-led training (vILT), Loom video walkthroughs, and Scribe (step-by-step screenshot guide) job aids.

Sample Scope Notice: Authored independently for portfolio review using generic customer support and operations scenarios. Zero confidential Carenet Health or client records are included.

1. ADDIE Instructional Design Architecture

Use tabs or Left/Right arrow keys to inspect each ADDIE phase, subject-matter expert (SME) review gates, and selected learning modalities.

Audience, Performance Gap & Learning Objective

  • Target Learners: Distributed remote specialists and points of contact routing live partner and customer escalations in customer relationship management (CRM) and ticketing systems.
  • Observed Bottleneck: Escalations bounce back between teams when specialists pass unverified symptoms or unedited AI summaries without checking source-of-truth dates and owner status first.
  • Measurable Objective: Given an ambiguous escalation or AI-drafted summary, learners apply a 4-step verification check, choose the matching triage route, and complete a confirmed warm handoff.

Blended Modality Architecture

  • Pre-Work (8 min async): Self-paced 1-page Scribe step-by-step screenshot guide paired with a 90-second Loom video walkthrough so live class focuses on active practice instead of passive lecture.
  • Live Virtual Session (45 min vILT): Guided sandbox navigation, AI-draft critique exercise, and live warm-handoff roleplay with immediate facilitator coaching.
  • On-the-Job Reinforcement: Interactive Escalation Triage Decision Tree linked directly inside Notion onboarding templates.

Content Development & AI Draft Validation

  • Drafting Workflow: Draft alternative practice scenarios and quiz distractors cleanly from SME bullet points using generative AI assistants.
  • Human Verification Gate: Every AI-assisted draft passes a mandatory accuracy, plain-language, inclusivity, and policy check against primary standard operating procedures (SOPs) before release (see AI Eval Rubric).
  • Accessibility & Clarity: Captioned video clips, scannable action headers, and non-jargon steps built for quick scanning under time pressure.

Distributed Remote Facilitation Plan

  • Cohort Format: Cohorts of 8 to 15 remote participants using structured chat prompts every 5 to 7 minutes to keep engagement balanced across time zones.
  • Facilitator Readiness: Timed cue sheet, screen-share sandbox checklist, plus async Loom + interactive quiz path for learners unable to attend live.
  • SME Co-Facilitation: Operations lead joins the final 10 minutes of pilot sessions to review live edge cases requiring SOP clarification.

Four-Level Evaluation Plan

  • Reaction (Session Close): 2-question end-of-workshop check on job-aid clarity and confidence applying the decision tree on live calls.
  • Learning Check (Immediate): Scenario quiz integrated in Section 4 below verifying judgment on unverified AI claims and warm handoffs.
  • Behavior & Quality Transfer (Weeks 1 - 2): Peer and QA spot-checks of live escalation tickets to verify missing intake fields decline and receiving owners are logged consistently.

2. Tangible Learning Artifact Preview (Job Aid & Micro-Video Script)

Example printable quick-reference card and 90-second video script handed directly to learners alongside the session.

Printable 1-Page Job Aid: Before You Escalate Checklist

  1. Confirm Source Record: Check primary portal/billing status and effective date match what the caller reported.
  2. Verify Any AI Summary: Delete invented percentages or service level agreement (SLA) claims not directly present in source notes.
  3. Package Reproduction Context: Include account ID (redacted where required), timestamp, expected vs. actual state, and impact.
  4. Secure Live Warm Handoff: Confirm named receiving owner directly and log owner + timestamp before updating waiting stakeholders.

90-Second Loom / Scribe Walkthrough Script

  • 0:00 - 0:20 Show broken ticket that bounced twice because the specialist pasted symptoms without verifying the effective date.
  • 0:20 - 0:55 Click step-by-step through Scribe capture points 1 to 3 comparing portal status against CRM notes and trimming an unsupported AI claim.
  • 0:55 - 1:30 Model logging named receiving owner + timestamp in the ticket, then direct learners to Question 1 in the quiz below.

3. Live Virtual Training (vILT) Facilitator Run-of-Show (45 Minutes)

Session timing, instructional modality, and facilitator cue guide.
Time Segment Modality / Tool Facilitator Cue & Learner Action
00:00 - 05:00 Opening Hook & Outcomes vILT + Chat Waterfall Ask: "What makes an escalated ticket bounce back?" Map learner answers onto the 4-step checklist.
05:00 - 15:00 Model the Standard Screen Share + Scribe Guide Walk through one conflicting-record sample live in sandbox view, pausing explicitly at date and owner verification steps.
15:00 - 28:00 Guided Case Practice + AI Check Breakout + AI Diff View Share an AI-drafted note containing an unsupported metric. Learners flag the gap and rewrite using source facts.
28:00 - 40:00 Warm-Handoff Roleplay Live Audio / Chat Practice Pairs practice a 2-minute live warm handoff: confirm receiving owner by name, state verified facts, log closure step.
40:00 - 45:00 Action Plan & Knowledge Check Notion Job Aid + Quiz Bookmark Notion checklist + decision tree; learners complete the interactive 3-question check below before wrap-up.
