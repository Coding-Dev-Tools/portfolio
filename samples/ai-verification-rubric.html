Generative AI Validation & Evidence-Gating Workflow

Generative AI tools accelerate first-pass instructional drafting, data curation, and investigative summaries, yet hallucinated metrics or vague jargon erode stakeholder trust immediately. This sample demonstrates my verification standard before any AI-assisted material ships.

1. Side-by-Side Comparison: Unverified Draft vs. Human-Verified Release

Switch between sample contexts to see how unsupported claims, policy risk, and tone issues are caught cleanly.

Raw Unverified AI Draft (Rejected)

"Our new clinic onboarding workflow reduces onboarding ramp time by 42% and ensures 100% error-free submissions. Always tell partners to email patient spreadsheets directly to support if the portal lags. Never bother clinical leads with edge cases."

  • Defect 1: Invented "42% ramp reduction" and "100% error-free" metrics not present in source SOPs.
  • Defect 2: Unsafe workaround (emailing spreadsheets with sensitive patient records outside approved intake).
  • Defect 3: Discourages escalation when edge cases genuinely require clinician or SME judgment.

Verified Audit-Ready Output (Approved)

"Use the approved CRM intake checklist and 1-page Scribe visual aid during clinic onboarding. If the portal fails to sync, capture a redacted screenshot plus timestamp and route directly through the encrypted support queue, escalating unresolved blockers to your on-duty lead."

  • Verified against current policy and privacy safeguards.
  • Actionable plain-language verbs replacing unverified metrics.
  • Clear escalation trigger when standard steps do not resolve cleanly.

Raw Unverified AI Draft (Rejected)

"Dataset A is completely clean across all 50 states and ready for customer rollout today. Only 2 records failed parsing, so we can skip schema validation."

  • Skipped denominator check (what % of required states were actually populated in source CSV?).
  • Ignored two failed parse rows without inspecting whether schema drift affects downstream joins.

Verified Audit-Ready Output (Approved)

"Evaluated 480 ingested clinic rows against required schema fields. Two records failed ISO date parsing in source feed X and remain quarantined with reproduction details attached. Hold production deployment until vendor confirms corrected date export."

  • Explicit observed sample count (`480 rows`) tied to real test output.
  • Isolated root cause cleanly before customer visibility.

Raw Unverified AI Draft (Rejected)

"Customer account #882 appears fraudulent based on elevated refund requests. Recommend immediate permanent suspension."

  • Jumped to conclusion without checking duplicate webhook retries or failed-card settlement logs.
  • No supporting evidence timeline for QA or compliance audit review.

Verified Audit-Ready Output (Approved)

"Reviewed three refund events on Account #882 (Nov 10 - Nov 12). Stripe payment logs confirm one duplicate client retry from an expired idempotency key rather than multiple distinct chargebacks. Verified customer identity history shows consistent billing region since onboarding; recommend closing as false positive and filing idempotency bug with Engineering."

  • Separated false alarm from genuine red flags with traced ledger evidence.
  • Identified technical edge case cleanly for Product and Engineering.
